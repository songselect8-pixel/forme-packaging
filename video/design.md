# Original website lookbook

12 seconds, 1280×720, 30 fps, silent, four original generated product photographs. Slow centered camera push, restrained half-second dissolves at 3, 6 and 9 seconds. Warm ivory, forest green and terracotta match the website. No factory footage, competitor media, invented capabilities, narration or stock footage. Use native video controls and a descriptive caption track. The HTML page supplies all headings and branding around the film rather than baking unreadable text into the moving imagery. Source and reproducible renderer: `prepare-media.py`, FFmpeg.
