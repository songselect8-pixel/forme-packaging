## Findings and fixes

- [P1, fixed] Mobile hero contrast. An inherited mobile `!important` gradient became light after palette replacement, reducing white-text contrast. A scoped dark-green mobile overlay and image treatment restored readability. Re-captured evidence: `output/comparison/home-mobile.jpg`.
- [P2, fixed] Quote-page checkbox overflow. The source's global input width expanded checkboxes to full width and pushed labels outside the viewport. Shared checkbox dimensions and flexible label text fix the cause. Re-tested all quote interactions and the six representative mobile routes; scroll width no longer exceeds viewport width.
- [P2, fixed] Product hero and quote layout proportions. Reference DOM evidence uses 1.2/.8 product tracks with a 56px gap and a 1248px outer container; the quote body is 1160px with a 60px gap. The implementation now follows these measurements. Re-captured evidence: `product-detail-desktop.jpg` and `quote-page-desktop.jpg` in `output/comparison/`.
- [P2, fixed] Catalog content density and desktop navigation sizing. Increased the featured-product reference text area to the source's approximate line density, restored the update line and matched desktop navigation text sizing. Re-captured evidence: `catalog-desktop.jpg` and `home-desktop.jpg`.

No actionable P0/P1/P2 findings remain for the approved scope.

## Required fidelity surfaces

- **Fonts/typography:** Local Manrope is an intentional style replacement. Hierarchy remains bold sans-serif headings, compact navigation, uppercase category labels and readable product copy. Wording/font changes produce minor line-wrap differences; these are expected under the authorized copy/style change.
- **Spacing/layout:** Desktop navigation is 68px and homepage hero 1000px at the test viewport. Homepage uses four product columns, catalog uses three; mobile uses one column. Original section order, 60 catalog slots, comparison panels, four process steps, FAQs, quote form and supporting routes are present. Product and quote proportions follow source measurements.
- **Colors/tokens:** Paper/white panels, forest-green accents and champagne hero details are the intentional replacement for black/gold. Mobile hero contrast was explicitly rechecked after the fix.
- **Images/assets:** All site photos are generated originals and locally served. All homepage product photos decoded successfully before captures. Existing product concepts may share a family image; no source photos, video, logo or hotlinked media are used. Film playback was verified at 1280px video width, 12 seconds, and actual playing state.
- **Copy/content:** Navigation and content topics follow the reference. Product descriptions and guides are rewritten. Company figures, credentials, reviews, contacts and commercial terms stay explicitly pending/project-specific. Planning calculator values are marked illustrative. No copied supplier identity, reference trackers or form/contact destinations remain.
